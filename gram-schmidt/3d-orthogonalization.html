Three starting vectors

Example vectors: v₁ = (2, 1, 0), v₂ = (1, 3, 1), v₃ = (2, 1, 6). The projection multipliers happen to equal 1 in this example, so the subtractions are especially easy to see. Gram–Schmidt works for any linearly independent starting vectors.
